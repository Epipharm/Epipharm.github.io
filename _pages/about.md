**Hyunsoo Rho** is an Assistant Professor in the College of Pharmacy at Ewha Womans University, where he leads the Epigenetic Pharmacology (EpiPharm) laboratory. His research investigates how cellular metabolism controls gene expression through epigenetic mechanisms and how these metabolism–epigenome links drive liver disease, including liver fibrosis and liver cancer.

#### Education

- Ph.D. in Biochemistry and Molecular Genetics, University of Illinois at Chicago (advisor: Nissim Hay)
- M.S. in Molecular Pharmacology, Seoul National University
- Bachelor of Pharmacy, Chung-Ang University

#### Previous Position

- Research Assistant Professor, Jeonbuk National University
